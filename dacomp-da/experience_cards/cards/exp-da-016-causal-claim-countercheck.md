# exp-da-016 Causal Claim Countercheck

## Experience
Before finalizing a causal claim, run a 3-step countercheck:
- Association check: confirm direction and magnitude across segments or periods.
- Rival explanation check: test at least one plausible alternative driver.
- Stability check: verify the claim still holds under a different cut (quartile, region, device, time bucket).

If evidence is insufficient, downgrade to "association" wording and state uncertainty.

## Minimal output
- Claim card: Claim, Evidence, Rival Tested, Stability Result, Confidence (high/medium/low).

## Common failure prevented
- Overstated causality and weak root-cause credibility.
