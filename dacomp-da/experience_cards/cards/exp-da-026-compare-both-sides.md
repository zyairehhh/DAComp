# exp-da-026 Compare Both Sides

## Experience
When the task asks to "compare" two groups, time periods, or options, rubrics expect both sides to be reported with numbers, not just one.
1. Identify the two (or more) entities being compared (e.g. Group A vs B, 2023 vs 2024, Option 1 vs 2).
2. For each metric that matters, report values for **all** sides (e.g. "A: 40%, B: 25%" not just "A is higher").
3. State the direction and magnitude of the difference (e.g. "A exceeds B by 15 percentage points").
4. Use the same definition and denominator for both sides so the comparison is fair.

## Checklist
- Comparison requested → list both (or all) sides.
- Each side → at least one number or metric.
- Difference → stated with sign and magnitude where relevant.

## Common failure prevented
- Accuracy/completeness deductions for one-sided or vague comparisons.
- Conclusiveness deductions when the reader cannot see both sides to judge the conclusion.
