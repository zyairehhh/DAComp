# exp-da-025 Deliverable Checklist

## Experience
Tasks often list multiple required outputs (e.g. "analyze X, identify Y, recommend Z"). Completeness deductions occur when any deliverable is missing or only partially addressed.
1. Before writing the final report, list each explicit deliverable from the task (bullet or numbered list).
2. For each deliverable, ensure there is a dedicated section or subsection with at least one concrete result (number, table, or finding).
3. If a deliverable cannot be answered from the data (e.g. data missing), state that explicitly and suggest what would be needed.
4. Do not merge or skip deliverables for brevity; each must have a traceable answer.

## Checklist
- [ ] All task verbs (analyze, identify, compare, recommend, …) mapped to a report section.
- [ ] Each section contains at least one verifiable output (number, table, or clear finding).
- [ ] No deliverable left with only "see above" or no new content.

## Common failure prevented
- Completeness deductions for missing or shallow treatment of a required deliverable.
