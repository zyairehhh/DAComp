# exp-da-015 Strategy Roadmap Operationalization

## Experience
Translate strategy into an executable 3-layer plan:
- Layer 1 (Objective): 2-4 measurable outcomes with target values.
- Layer 2 (Workstreams): each objective mapped to concrete initiatives.
- Layer 3 (Execution contract): owner, start/end timing, KPI, and risk guardrail.

Every recommendation should include at least one measurable KPI and one trigger threshold.

## Minimal output
- A roadmap table with columns: Objective, Initiative, Owner, Timeline, KPI Target, Guardrail.

## Common failure prevented
- High-level advice that cannot be executed or monitored.
