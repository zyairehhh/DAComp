# DA Trend & Relationship

## Workflow

### 1. Trend Analysis
- Aggregate by time grain.
- Identify direction, inflection, and seasonality.

### 2. Relationship/Test
- Compute correlation or required test.
- Report effect size + p-value.
- Interpret practical meaning.
