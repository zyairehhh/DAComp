# DA Effect Modeling

## Workflow

### 1. Specify Model
- Outcome, key predictors, controls.
- Model type (OLS/logit/LOESS).

### 2. Fit and Interpret
- Report coefficients or marginal effects with units.
- Explain practical meaning.

### 3. Robustness
- Note assumptions and limitations.
