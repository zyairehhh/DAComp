# exp-da-030 Compare at least two distinct evaluation paradigms before finalizing

## Experience
* **Design alternative methods:** Create at least two fundamentally different evaluation approaches (e.g., rule-based fixed weights vs. statistical Z-score ranking; weighted sum vs. TOPSIS multi-criteria decision analysis).
* **Apply both methods to the full dataset:** Run the complete analysis pipeline for each method to generate final scores and classifications.
* **Create a cross-method comparison table:** Show, for key entities (e.g., the 'Excellent' group), their scores and classification under each method. Calculate agreement statistics (e.g., top-K overlap, correlation).
* **Justify final selection:** Based on the comparison, explicitly state which method is chosen for the final plan and why (e.g., better fairness, simpler interpretation, higher robustness).

## Checklist
- Design 2+ distinct evaluation methods
- Run full analysis for each method
- Create cross-method comparison table
- Justify final method selection

## Common failure prevented
Prevents delivering a single evaluation method without demonstrating its validity against alternatives, failing to meet rubric requirements for method comparison.
