# exp-da-008 Quantify qualitative goals with specific KPIs and thresholds

## Experience
- Operationalize each qualitative goal into 2-3 measurable KPIs using available data.
- Define specific, numerical targets or thresholds for each KPI (e.g., 'satisfaction score > 4.0').
- Calculate the historical baseline for each KPI from the data to show the starting point.
- Create a monitoring plan that includes the KPI, its current value, target, and a defined alert trigger.
- In your strategy proposal, link your initiatives directly to moving these specific KPIs.

## Checklist
- Translate each qualitative goal into concrete, measurable KPIs
- Set a clear numerical target for each KPI
- Calculate the historical baseline for each KPI
- Design a simple dashboard or alert logic for monitoring
- Link strategic initiatives to impacting specific KPIs

## Common failure prevented
Prevents vague, non-actionable recommendations by ensuring proposals have clear success measures and monitoring mechanisms.
