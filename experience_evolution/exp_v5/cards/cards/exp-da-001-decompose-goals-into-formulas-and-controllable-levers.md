# exp-da-001 Decompose goals into formulas and controllable levers

## Experience
* **Step 1:** Paraphrase the user's goal in your own words (e.g., 'maximize annual earnings per kilometer driven').
* **Step 2:** Break this goal down into its direct, measurable components using a formula (e.g., **Earnings per km = (Total Earnings) / (Total Distance)**).
* **Step 3:** For each component, list the levers a user can control (e.g., vehicle choice, service quality) and external factors (e.g., time, location, trip characteristics).
* **Step 4:** Design analyses to quantify the impact of each lever and factor on the formula's components.
* **Step 5:** Ensure your final report answers 'so what?' by translating findings into concrete, prioritized actions tied to the controllable levers.

## Checklist
- Decompose the user's goal into a formula or key metrics
- Identify controllable levers and external factors for each metric
- Design analyses to measure each lever's impact on the key metrics

## Common failure prevented
Producing a narrow, incomplete answer because the analysis was limited to surface-level correlations without connecting to the underlying goal mechanics.
