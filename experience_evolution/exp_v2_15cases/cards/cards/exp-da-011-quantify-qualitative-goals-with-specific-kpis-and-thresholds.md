# exp-da-011 Quantify qualitative goals with specific KPIs and thresholds

## Experience
- **Identify the qualitative directive** in the task (e.g., 'avoid risk', 'optimize strategy').
- **Define specific, measurable KPIs** that operationalize the goal (e.g., 'avoid models with depreciation ratio > X%', 'prioritize configurations with turnover < Y days').
- **Set thresholds or targets** for each KPI based on data or domain knowledge.
- **Generate recommendations that explicitly reference these KPIs and thresholds**.
- **Use directive language** (e.g., 'avoid', 'prioritize', 'recommend') in the recommendations.

## Checklist
- Parse task for qualitative goals
- Define corresponding quantitative KPIs
- Set measurable thresholds or targets
- Generate actionable recommendations
- Use directive language

## Common failure prevented
Providing only descriptive findings without actionable, directive recommendations tied to measurable business outcomes.
